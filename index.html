How A Stump Job Runs

Roots to level ground, in four passes.

01

Clear & Mark

Expose the root flare, flag sprinkler lines and cables nearby.

02

Grind Down

Wheel works 4–6" below grade, then chases the surface roots.

03

Backfill

Mulch and soil brought level with the surrounding lawn.

04

Haul or Leave

Mulch hauled off-site, or piled for your beds — your call.
